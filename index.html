Privacy Policy

App: UTracker: Movies & Books

Developer: BlaizTM

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how UTracker: Movies & Books handles information when you use the application. The app helps users discover and track movies and books. According to its current design, it does not require user accounts and does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

UTracker is designed to store your saved collections and app preferences locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include movie and book lists, ratings, notes, and tracking progress.

The app does not currently use a developer-operated account system or backend database to store these collections. You do not need to create an account to use the app.

Only use of features actually present in the released app is covered by this description. Any optional profile or email fields, if present, should be reviewed to determine whether their information remains on-device or is transmitted.

3. Movie and Book Information Providers

UTracker uses the following third-party APIs to retrieve movie and book information:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): https://www.themoviedb.org/ and its API documentation .
  • Open Library: https://openlibrary.org/ and its API documentation .
  • Google Books API: https://books.google.com/ and its API documentation .

When the app requests information from these services, the request may include a search term, book or movie identifier, or other parameters needed to retrieve the requested information.

These services may also receive technical information associated with network requests, such as an IP address, request time, and technical request metadata. The exact information received depends on the API, connection method, and provider's systems.

These providers operate independently of BlaizTM and have their own terms and privacy practices. Please review their applicable policies and terms:

  • TMDB Privacy Policy and TMDB API Terms .
  • Open Library and its API usage information .
  • Google Privacy Policy and Google Books API Terms .

4. App Version and Update Checks

UTracker contacts a developer-controlled domain to check whether an app update is available. For this purpose, the app version may be sent to that domain.

The version information is used to support the update-checking function. The developer does not use a dashboard or other interface to view this version information.

Depending on the server configuration and hosting provider, technical request information, such as an IP address or server logs, may also be processed automatically. The actual information retained by the server depends on its configuration.

The update check is separate from the local movie and book collections. According to the current design, those collections are not uploaded to the developer's domain.

5. Accounts, Advertising, and Analytics

According to the current app design, UTracker does not require accounts or sign-in and does not integrate advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

This statement applies only while those integrations remain absent from the released app and its included software development kits.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the app is used to provide its features, including:

  • Displaying movie and book information.
  • Organizing saved collections and tracking progress.
  • Remembering supported preferences on your device.
  • Checking whether an app update is available.

7. Data Sharing

Saved collections are designed to remain on your device and are not uploaded to a developer-operated collection database. However, API requests are sent to the relevant external providers, and the app version is sent to the developer's update-checking domain.

BlaizTM does not control the independent providers' handling of information they receive. Their own policies describe their practices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until it is removed or otherwise cleared. Where supported, you can delete saved items using the app's controls, clear the app's storage through your device settings, or uninstall the app.

These actions may permanently delete local information. They do not necessarily remove technical logs already retained by an API provider, the update-checking host, or a device backup service. Contact the relevant provider for questions about information held by that provider.

9. Data Security

Local information is subject to the security of your device and the app's storage implementation. Network communications depend on the app's implementation and the security measures used by the relevant providers. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

UTracker is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed to target children. Its actual target audience and age rating are determined separately through the applicable app-store settings.
